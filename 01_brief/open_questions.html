1.2

Hotel category and operator

Needed to start

The star rating and the operator decide room sizes, corridor widths, lift numbers and back-of-house areas. International operators issue their own design standards, which override general rules of thumb.

Please send
Target category (e.g. 4★ / 5★), operator or brand if chosen, and their standards book. Also whether it is a classic hotel, an aparthotel or serviced apartments.
1.3

Room mix and room sizes

Needed to start

The number of keys and the room types set the floor plate depth and the structural grid.

Please send
Total keys, room types with target net areas (m²), number of suites, share of accessible rooms.
1.4

Please send
Restaurants and bars (seats), conference (largest room and total m²), spa / wellness / pool, fitness, rooftop use, ground-floor retail.
