Assumptions to confirm

Permitting

  • Procedure follows the Building Act 283/2021 Sb. as amended (single povolení záměru, DPZ per vyhláška 131/2024 Sb.). Confirm with the local architect.
  • Which office decides: district building office (Praha 4) or a MHMP department. Ask at the pre-application consultation.
  • Whether EIA screening applies (parking count, built-up area). The answer changes the timeline the most.
  • MPP court review MS Praha 11 A 24/2026 is pending. If the plan is annulled, the rules in rules.yaml change.

Project data

  • Parking zone 02 is ASSUMED in rules.yaml. Verify it on the Atlas Prahy zone map.
  • The S and E plot edges are ASSUMED to face public space (PSP §22).
  • No client brief in 01_brief/ yet. Room mix, budget and dates are open.
  • Metro C/D protection zone and the heritage-reserve view impact are flagged as consultations, not yet checked.
