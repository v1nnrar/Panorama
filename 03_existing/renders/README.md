## What the renders show (first read — to be verified)

Storey counts are estimates from the images, not from drawings.

- **Diagrid tower** (01, 02, 05, 06): the tallest building in the scheme, about 24–26 storeys.
  A square-plan tower with a white diagonal exoskeleton over recessed balconies, a fully
  glazed double-height base, and PV panels plus a green roof on top.
- **Residential tower** (01, 02, 04): about 14–16 storeys of cantilevered floor slabs with
  planters and white vertical fins. It rises out of a **terraced block** of about 7–8 storeys
  with stepped, planted terraces and timber-toned soffits and columns. There are PV panels on
  the tower roof and on the terraced block.
- **Office block** (01, 03, 05): a long, low slab of about 8–9 storeys along the street, with
  horizontal white bands and louvres, a recessed and colonnaded ground floor, and green
  rooftop terraces. In 03 it faces a landscaped park.
- **Landscape:** a raised, planted courtyard between the buildings, a landscaped park on the
  frontage, and green roofs throughout.
- **Context:** the large flat-roofed shopping centre and the glass high-rises of Pankrác
  behind; a group of rounded, green-roofed buildings to one side; prefab housing blocks
  across the street.

### Things to reconcile before modelling

- The renders do not all appear to show the same design stage: massing, cladding colour
  (cream in 01, white in 05/06) and how the towers meet their bases vary between views.
- Render 02 shows a dark-glazed block with a bronze roof edge in the right foreground. It is
  unclear whether that block is part of the scheme or a neighbour.
- Exact storey counts, floor-to-floor heights and footprints need drawings or the brief
  (`01_brief/`).
