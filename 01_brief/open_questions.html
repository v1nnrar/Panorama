1.1

The existing hotel: demolish or refurbish? And is the new building a hotel or residential?

Start here

The Panorama Hotel at Milevská 7 stands on the plot today. What happens to it, and what the new building is used for, decide almost everything else on this page. Pick the answers below to see the follow-up questions for each.

A · The existing hotel building

Pick an answer to see the follow-up questions.

  • Which buildings go? The hotel tower only, or other buildings on the plot as well? Our model currently keeps the corner office building E (parcel 1133/12).
  • Demolition permit and timing. Has a demolition permit been applied for or issued? When does the hotel close?
  • Hazardous materials survey. Buildings of this age often contain asbestos and other materials that need specialist removal. Is there a survey?
  • Basement and foundations. Should we try to reuse the existing basement, foundations or utility connections?
  • Existing drawings. Original and as-built plans, sections and structural drawings, in any format.
  • Building survey. Condition of the structure, facade, building services and fire safety. Can the structure carry extra floors?
  • How deep a refurbishment? Facade only, new interiors, new building services, or an extension with added floors or a new wing?
  • Operation during works. Does the hotel stay open, fully or partly, while we build?
  • Heritage protection. Is the building or its surroundings protected in any way?
  • We can compare both. We can model a refurbishment and a new building side by side, with areas and costs for each. For a realistic refurbishment option we still need the existing drawings and any building survey.
  • What would tip the decision? Budget, timeline, keeping the hotel open, or the size of the new building?
B · The new building is…

Pick an answer to see the follow-up questions.

For a mix

  • How is it split? Which buildings or floors are hotel and which are apartments, roughly in m² or number of keys and units?
  • Shared or separate? Separate entrances, lifts and parking for hotel guests and residents, or shared services such as the gym or restaurant?

Hotel

  • Which buildings and floors are hotel? Our model now guesses the front slab C1/C2 and the diagrid tower A. A marked-up render is enough.
  • What kind of hotel, and who runs it? How many stars (3★, 4★, 5★)? A chain such as Marriott or Accor, a local operator, or Property Solutions itself? A chain will give us a design manual we must follow. A classic hotel, an aparthotel or serviced apartments?
  • Rooms and sizes. Total number of rooms, room types with target sizes in m² (for example standard 25–30 m², superior 30–35 m², suite 50 m² +), how many suites and penthouse suites, and the share of accessible rooms.
  • What else should guests find in the hotel, besides their rooms? For example: a restaurant or bar (and for how many people), meeting or conference rooms (how big is the largest event), a spa or pool, a gym, a rooftop bar, shops on the ground floor. Tell us which you want and roughly how big.
  • Back of house. Kitchen size, laundry in-house or outsourced, staff numbers, deliveries and waste.
